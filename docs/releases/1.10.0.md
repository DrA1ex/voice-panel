# VoicePanel 1.10.0

Native macOS dictation from your menu bar: hold **Control + Option + Space**,
speak, release, and paste the completed transcript.

## Downloads

- **Apple Silicon:** `VoicePanel-1.10.0-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel:** `VoicePanel-1.10.0-x86_64.dmg`
- **Requirements:** macOS 14 or later; Microphone permission. Apple Speech and
  Apple live drafts also require Speech Recognition permission.

Open the DMG, drag VoicePanel to Applications, and launch it. The app lives in
the menu bar without a Dock icon. Choose your microphone, language, and engine
in **Settings → General**.

**These packages are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized by Apple.** If macOS
blocks the first launch, Control-click VoicePanel and choose Open, or use
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to
launch it. See [installation troubleshooting](https://github.com/DrA1ex/voice-panel/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#macos-blocks-the-app).

## Included features

- Global push-to-talk and menu-controlled recording with an editable result.
- Apple on-device Speech and downloadable local Whisper, GigaAM v3,
  Qwen3-ASR, and Parakeet engines.
- Live draft text, recording waveform, and progress during model preparation
  and final transcription.
- Audio-file import through the menu or drag-and-drop.
- Recognition profiles, microphone environments, and model/pipeline benchmarks.
- Encrypted transcript history with Touch ID or password unlock.
- Independent panel/window appearance and configurable completion behavior.

This build includes fixes for microphone initialization when input and output
sample rates differ, live transcript scrolling, per-segment draft styling,
full transcript pending drafts, and Apple live drafts across utterance restarts.

## Privacy and known limitations

Downloaded recognition models run on your Mac; model downloads need a network
connection. Apple Speech requires on-device recognition by default; disabling
that requirement allows macOS to use Apple servers. Models
are downloaded separately and are not included in the DMGs. History defaults to
encrypted with seven-day retention. Optional audio debugging is disabled by
default; enabling it saves unencrypted local WAV files.

Qwen3-ASR 1.7B is experimental. Apple Speech needs on-device recognition support
for the chosen language. Use the native build for your Mac; the Intel package
is not recommended for Apple Silicon through Rosetta.
